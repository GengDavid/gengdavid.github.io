# Monochrome revision · 2026-09-22

The user requested a simpler academic style, specifically rejecting the previous blue, green, and beige palette.

- White page and surfaces; near-black headings, body text, and links; neutral gray metadata and dividers.
- One local sans-serif font stack throughout. Body text is 15px, publication titles 19px, and author lists 12px at desktop and phone widths.
- About and news remain side by side on desktop. Research retains three columns, and publications retain two columns.
- No colored research cards, filled news/contact panels, offset portrait frame, decorative section numbers, or card movement on hover.
- Text links retain underlines where they appear in paragraphs. Active filters and keyboard focus use black and white.
- The original photograph remains unmodified and has no location caption. The About Me heading and current research categories are retained.

References inspected: [Jon Barron](https://jonbarron.info/), including his [public stylesheet](https://jonbarron.info/stylesheet.css), and [Deepak Pathak](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dpathak/). The reference informed the restrained typography and emphasis on research content. This implementation uses its own grid layout and CSS; it does not copy a template or publication content.

Current renders and browser checks are saved in `previews/`. These are local previews; the revision has not been published by the assistant.
